# Hidden languages

Translations made by Commet's community, kept as they were and left out of
the build: they cover Commet's strings, not Rooster's, so shipping them
would show half of the app in English. `intl_pt_PT.arb` is Commet's
European Portuguese; Rooster's Portuguese is Brazilian
(`rooster/assets/l10n/intl_pt.arb`).

To bring one back, see [Adding a language](../../../docs/localization.md#adding-a-language).
