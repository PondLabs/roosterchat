<p>Cockhouse is a client for Matrix focused on providing a feature rich experience while maintaining a simple interface.
    The goal is to build a secure, privacy respecting app without compromising on the features you have come to expect
    from a modern chat client.</p>

<h3>Feature Rich</h3>
<p>Cockhouse aims to provide all the functionality you have come to expect from a modern chat app, without compromising
    security.</p>
<ul>
    <li>🖼️ GIF Search</li>
    <li>☎️ Calls + Voice Chats</li>
    <li>🧵 Threads</li>
    <li>🔍 Encrypted Message Search</li>
    <li>🔗 URL Preview</li>
    <li>😀 Custom Emoji + Stickers</li>
    <li>🚀 Spaces</li>
    <li>📳 Push Notifications</li>
    <li>🔒 End to End Encryption</li>
    <li>➕ And more!</li>
</ul>

<h3>Your space to connect</h3>

<p>In addition to text and calls, Cockhouse provides additional tools to help you hang out and connect with your friends.
    Easily plan events with a shared calendar, and upload all the photos to a shared album!</p>


<h3> Seamless multi-account support</h3>

<p> Cockhouse was built from the ground up with multi-account support in mind, with a unique approach which doesn't require
    manually switching account.</p>

<p>All your accounts are logged in simultaneously, seamlessly blending together so you can focus on the conversation.
</p>


<h3>We are Open Source</h3>
<p> Cockhouse is built and maintained by a passionate group of volunteers. We are always grateful for any help we can get!
</p>
