# Rooster brand

> **Rooster is where your crew talks, plays and hangs out.**

When a design question comes up, check the answer against that sentence first.

![lockup with tagline](preview/lockup-tagline.png)

## The idea

The mascot is a little unserious and the product is serious. The design sits between the two.

The rooster is the one who wakes everyone up and calls them in. It is a bit loud, a bit ridiculous and completely sure of itself, and it always has its headphones on, because it is always in the call. The name says that plainly, so the logo, the stickers and the interface can carry the personality without explaining it.

Rooster should feel warm, playful, a little irreverent, confident, internet-native and a bit weird. It should not feel corporate, like enterprise SaaS, childish, edgy for its own sake, sexual, crypto, "AI startup", or like a Discord clone. Stay away from the visual language of Discord, Slack, Teams, Zoom, Linear and Notion.

The app is still a place: spaces are houses, channels are rooms, and people come and go. Use that as a light layer of personality in the copy. Don't force it onto every element.

## Logo

The mark is a rooster's head in profile, facing right, with headphones on. A big comb of rounded lobes fans back from the crown. The whole head is feather white. The headphone band arcs from the big ear cup on the cheek up over the head, and the face has a dark eye, a yolk beak and a comb-red wattle. A small arched door sits at the bottom of the neck, the one trace of the house the brand started as.

The art itself is [`src/mark.png`](src/mark.png): four flat colours on a transparent background. The build traces it into vector shapes, so every size and colourway comes from that one drawing.

![icon family](preview/icon-family.png)

The mark comes in these forms:

1. **On ink.** The app icon, and the default everywhere.
2. **On cream.** For light surfaces and the light lockup.
3. **On comb red.** For loud moments: stickers, event banners, the store feature graphic. The comb turns a lighter red (`#FF8466`) here so it doesn't vanish.
4. **One colour.** The silhouette with the features cut out of it, on ink or comb red, square or round. For stamps, embroidery and single-ink printing. Android's themed icon and status-bar icon use this form.

**Small sizes.** The mark holds up unchanged down to 16 px. Below 48 px, the tile's margin shrinks so the head fills more of it.

![icon sizes 16, 32, 48, 128](preview/icon-sizes.png)

**Don'ts.** Don't redraw the mark into anything phallic, recolour the comb away from comb red (except on a comb-red tile and in one-colour use), take the headphones off, add gradients or shadows, or stretch it. Keep clear space around it of at least the beak's length.

Everything in `logo/` and `merch/`, and every platform icon in `rooster/`, is generated by [`src/build_brand.py`](src/build_brand.py). To change the mark, change `src/mark.png`; for the rest, change the script, not the output:

```sh
python3 -m venv /tmp/brand && /tmp/brand/bin/pip install fonttools pillow numpy potracer
/tmp/brand/bin/python docs/brand/src/build_brand.py            # docs/brand only
/tmp/brand/bin/python docs/brand/src/build_brand.py --install  # plus the app's icons
```

It needs `google-chrome` (for rasterising) and ImageMagick's `convert` on the PATH. `--install` writes:

- the Flutter assets;
- web favicons, PWA and maskable icons, and the web splash;
- the Windows `.ico` and the tray's idle icon (`rooster_tray_idle`);
- the Linux hicolor and Flatpak icons;
- the Android launcher icons, adaptive foreground, themed monochrome and notification icons, and the adaptive background colour.

## Colour

![palette](preview/palette.png)

Six colours, warm and flat: ink and cream for the ground, comb red and yolk for the rooster, stone and spruce for quiet moments.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#211614` | The headphones, eye and door; the app-icon tile; text on light |
| Comb | `#E8382A` | The comb and wattle, and the primary accent: buttons, selection, focus, hang up |
| Yolk | `#F89B17` | The beak; highlights, away |
| Cream | `#F7EDE1` | Light tile and background, text on dark |
| Stone | `#8D8178` | Secondary text, the tagline |
| Spruce | `#2E3B33` | Quiet dark accent |

The rooster's face is `#FCF8F1`. Status colours the app already uses (green for online and speaking, blue for links) stay as they are; they are functional, not brand.

Rules:

- **Comb red is the one loud colour.** Use it for the primary action and the comb. Don't flood whole screens with it.
- **No purple, no SaaS blue, no gradients.** Flat colour.
- **Dark mode comes first.** It is designed first and checked first.

In the app, the built-in themes (`tiamat/lib/config/style/theme_dark.dart`, `theme_light.dart`) use:

| Role | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation (behind panels) | `#1B1613` | `#E4D7C6` |
| Surface | `#2A221E` | `#FBF6EF` |
| Container lowest / low / normal / high | `#161210` `#1F1916` `#241D1A` `#362C27` | `#FFFCF7` `#F6EFE5` `#F0E7DA` `#E9DECF` |
| Text | `#F5EBDD` | `#231A16` |
| Primary / on primary | `#E8382A` / `#FFF7EE` | `#C9452B` / `#FFFFFF` |
| Links | `#8FB4DA` | `#3D6B98` |

By day the primary is a little deeper (`#C9452B`), so the label on a filled button keeps at least 3:1 contrast in both themes. `unit_test/filled_icon_button_contrast_test.dart` checks this. Don't lighten the dark primary: cream labels drop below 3:1 fast. The AMOLED and "follow system colours" themes are left as they were.

## Typography

- **Sora** (`rooster/assets/font/sora`, OFL, variable): the wordmark in ExtraBold (800, slightly tightened), and display and marketing headlines, from Regular to ExtraBold. It is contemporary and friendly, with rounded bowls.
- **Nunito Sans** (bundled, variable): headings and UI emphasis in the app.
- **Roboto**: body text, as the app has it today.
- **JetBrains Mono**: code.

**Writing the name.** Write "Rooster", capitalised, in running text, titles and the wordmark. Merch can use caps (ROOSTER). The tagline is *Talk. Play. Hang out.*, in Sora Medium and Stone.

## Stickers and illustration

The rooster's head is also the sticker set: the same head with different faces. Winking, crowing, sunglasses with music notes, asleep (zZ), annoyed, confused (?). Keep the comb, the headphones and the colours; change only the eye, the beak and one small sign beside the head.

The style is simple shapes, strong silhouettes and flat palette colours. The rooster is confident and friendly, and a little stupid in an endearing way. It is not a farm logo, a fighting cock, or a children's cartoon. The humour comes from the situation it is in: DJing, asleep in a call, staring at a screen, talking into a mic.

Avoid people shaking hands, floating blobs, isometric offices, gradient landscapes, glassmorphism and stock photos. Never draw anything sexual.

## Icons and motifs

UI icons are rounded, slightly chunky, highly legible and consistent (Material Symbols Rounded suits this). One brand motif recurs, sparingly:

- **The comb.** Rounded lobes. It can shape a mention or knock badge, a speaking meter (rounded bars), a loading indicator (dots taking turns), or a separator ornament. Don't use the whole rooster head as a UI icon.

## Motion

Motion should be lively and organic, never flashy.

- Sound-wave and speaking indicators move like voices, not metronomes.
- The rooster can bob its head to the beat when the DJ booth is playing. Once in a while, not constantly.
- The DJ booth gets more energy than anything else.
- Short easing (150 to 250 ms), no bouncy overshoot on functional UI, and respect reduced-motion settings.

## Voice and copy

Rooster talks like a person talking to their friends. It is for **people**, not users. Groups are **crews**, **groups** or **flocks**, depending on context.

Good: *Your people are here.* · *Come hang out.* · *Your house. Your rules.* · *Bring the whole flock.* · *Pull up a chair.* · *Come on in.* · *Stay a while.* · *Make yourself at home.* · *Talk. Play. Hang out.*

Never: *Revolutionizing community communication.* · *Enterprise-grade communication for modern teams.* · *Seamless omnichannel communication.*

**Humour is seasoning.** Most interface text should stay plain and functional: "Mute", "Leave call" and "Upload failed, try again" are correct. Personality belongs in empty states, welcomes and small moments:

| Moment | Copy |
|---|---|
| Login | Come on in. |
| New room | Welcome to #room! Make yourself at home. |
| Empty voice room | It's quiet in here. Pull up a chair. |
| Alone in a call | You're alone in here. |
| Nobody online | Everyone's out. |
| Someone pings you | Someone's at the door. / Knock knock. |
| Joining a server | Join the crew. |

The humour works on three levels:

1. **Normal.** You can use Rooster without noticing the mascot: "I'm in the call."
2. **Playful.** Rooster references for people who know the brand: "The flock is online."
3. **Cheeky.** Only in marketing, Easter eggs, merch and community jokes: "Rise and shine, the flock is up."

Level 3 never appears in the product's core UI. The brand has to sit comfortably on GitHub, Hacker News, Reddit, in app stores and at conferences without looking like a novelty app.

## Merch

![tee](preview/tee-on-dark.png)

The mark, big, on the chest. `merch/tee-on-dark.svg` and `tee-on-light.svg` are full colour, and `tee-one-colour.svg` is the one-colour form for single-ink screen printing.

It also works for caps (the mark embroidered), hoodies, die-cut stickers (the app icon and the sticker faces), enamel pins (ink, comb red, yolk and white enamel) and mugs.

## What is renamed, and what is not

Everything that is the app's own is **Rooster**. That covers the display name everywhere a person sees it, the app ID `com.pondlabs.rooster`, the `rooster` executable, the data directories, the `rooster/` folder and Dart package, and the release asset names. Installs from before the renames (Rooster, and Commet before it) update in place and bring their data with them.

What keeps Commet's name is the Matrix protocol (`chat.commet.*` event types), the `chat.commet` URL scheme, the web build's database name, and Commet's own services and attribution. Renaming those would break other clients, links and existing users. The GitHub repo is `PondLabs/roosterchat`; the old `PondLabs/roscord` URLs redirect to it.

The full list, and how earlier installs are brought across, is in [`docs/adr/0003-rename-to-rooster.md`](../adr/0003-rename-to-rooster.md), which builds on [`0002-rename-to-rooster.md`](../adr/0002-rename-to-rooster.md).
