Rooster

Talk. Play. Hang out.

Always-on voice rooms for your crew, with screen share, a soundboard and a DJ booth. Runs on Matrix, so you can host your own server, federate with friends and keep it end-to-end encrypted.

Download Open in browser

Voice rooms that stay open

Drop in, drop out. Rooms don't ring, they just are. Per-person volume, noise suppression and push to talk, on desktop and in the browser.

Share your screen

Share a window or the whole screen, with its audio, and watch what everyone else is sharing in a grid.

Soundboard

Add a clip from a file or a link, trim it, and fire it into the call. Every space gets its own board.

DJ booth

Queue songs from your own files and play them into the room, in sync, for everyone.

Your house, your rules

It's Matrix underneath: run your own server or use any public one, bring your existing account, and talk to people on other clients.

Free and open source

AGPL-3.0. No accounts to sell, no ads, no telemetry. Read the code, build it yourself, or tell us what's broken.
